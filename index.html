About me

Still learning. Still asking questions.

I've had the opportunity to work across 5+ different industries, and I've learned not to take a brief at face value. More often than not, it's a starting point rather than the answer — and getting to the real problem usually takes a fair number of questions, and a few wrong turns along the way.

What I try to pay attention to is where people hesitate, what they reach for under pressure, and what makes something feel trustworthy enough to actually use. I stay close to the work end-to-end, from early conversations to the handoff — it's the only way I've found to keep learning.

How I work

A few things I try to hold onto

These are the things I keep coming back to, project after project.

01

Understand before I design

I'd rather spend a week understanding a problem than a week designing the wrong solution to it. That time upfront tends to pay for itself later.

02

I try to be honest about trade-offs

Every design leaves something out. I'd rather name that trade-off out loud so the team can weigh in, instead of quietly deciding it for everyone.

03

I think about the hardest moment, not the easiest

Field heat, patchy GPS, a night shift, a distressed caller — if it holds up there, it'll hold up on a good day too.
